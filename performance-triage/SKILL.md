# Performance triage

Choose representative pages and test environments. Collect Lighthouse lab results in an authorized browser. Distinguish lab data from field Core Web Vitals. Inspect image dimensions, format, lazy loading, JS budget, critical CSS, fonts, caching and third-party scripts. Record each measurement with URL, date, device and profile. Propose optimizations ranked by expected impact and confidence. Re-run under comparable conditions to verify.
